# Artwork sources

The initial reference library is stored in `data/library.json`. The complete source/license record is stored in `data/sources.json`.

The library was built around Wikimedia Commons line-art collections and public-domain / CC0 material. Examples of the source collections researched include:

- Line art of horses
- Line art of dogs
- Line art of birds
- Line art of fish
- Line art of buildings
- Line art of weapons
- Line art of people
- Line art of plants
- Line art of vehicles
- Line art of dragons
- Line art of objects

The selected Pearson Scott Foresman assets are explicitly marked public domain on Wikimedia Commons. Several older drawings are also explicitly marked public domain, and the winged-dragon reference is CC0.

Important: Wikimedia Commons is a repository containing files under different licenses. Keep the individual source page with each asset and re-check the license before distributing a production copy. The app's `data/sources.json` file exists so this can be audited later without manually searching for every image again.
